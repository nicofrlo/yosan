Track your spending on iPhone without giving anyone your bank login.

Privacy policy

Yosan collects no data and never goes online: no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your expenses never leave your iPhone unless you send them.

Your data stays on your iPhone

  • What you add or import is stored by Yosan on your iPhone and nowhere else. Nobody else can see it, including its developer.
  • Your iPhone’s backups (iCloud or computer) include it, like other apps’ data.
  • Deleting Yosan deletes all of it. To keep a copy, use Back up everything in Settings first.

Automatic logging

  • Apple Pay: your Shortcuts automation hands Yosan each payment’s amount and merchant.
  • Bank notifications: your automation hands Yosan the notification’s text. Yosan reads it on your iPhone, keeps only the amount and the merchant, and never stores the text.
  • Statements you import are read on your iPhone. Backups and exports go only where you send them.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: what you log is handed to Yosan on your iPhone, like a typed expense.

Support

Questions, a problem or an idea: write to yosan.budget@gmail.com.
